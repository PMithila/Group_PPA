About Us

Sunrise Community Learning is a local, non-profit educational center offering vocational training, youth programs and evening classes. We focus on hands-on learning, mentorship and community involvement.

Programs

Vocational Training

Short courses in trades, IT basics and hospitality — practical skills for immediate employment.

Youth Development

After-school activities, coding clubs and leadership workshops for ages 8–18.

Adult Learning

Evening literacy, language classes and computer skills to support lifelong learning.

Community Projects

Volunteer-driven projects connecting learners to local employers and causes.
